# The Craft of Computing

<div class="rule"></div>

---

# What is this course about?

* First course in the Information Sciences major and minor
* Hands-on exploration of computing across three facets:
  * Command line interface
  * Python programming
  * Microcontrollers (ESP32)
* Goal: move from passive tech consumers to active creators and problem solvers
* Emphasis on teamwork, self-directed learning, and getting to know classmates

---

<!-- _class: section -->

# Course Objectives & Units
<div class="rule"></div>

---

# Course Objectives

* **Execute** basic computing tasks by following guidance or consulting resources
* **Foster** an inclusive learning environment and responsible conduct
* **Examine & troubleshoot** commands and code
* **Design & implement** new applications through tinkering and thoughtful design
* **Introspect** on learning and know when to seek help

---

# Course Units

1. Computing with the command line
2. Exploring Python
3. Interfacing with Microcontrollers

# In-class Reflections

- Individual assessment
- Two reflections: start of course (week 2) and end (week 14 / assessment week)
- First: views on computing, what they hope to learn
- Second: revisit those views, reflect on challenges and how they were addressed

---

# Group Presentation

- Group assessment
- Class time will be dedicated to prepare presentation

---

# Group Projects

- Group assessment (groups of 3 or 4)
- One open-ended project per unit — 3 total
- Class time dedicated to work on the project and seek instructor help

---

# Activity Reports

- Group assessments
- Based on in-class activities in lab sessions
- Best 3 contribute 3% each

---

# Test

- Individual, in-class assessment
- conducted after unit 2 has been completed (week 11 or 12)
- Pen and paper assessment

---


# Learning Journal

- Individual, online journal built from Markdown files using a static site generator
- Minimum 6 entries required:
  - 1 on the group presentation
  - 3 on the three group projects
  - 2 on troubleshooting during class activities
- Students may write more entries if they wish

---

# Class Participation

- Assessed on facilitating collaborative learning, responsible use of facilities, and consistent engagement
- Proactively seeking academic support counts as active engagement

# Policies

<div class="rule"></div>

---

# Attendance Policies

* 100% attendance is expected; 80% attendance is the minimum threshold to be eligible for a passing grade
* No late arrivals
* You will be marked absent if you do not come on time after in-class breaks

---

# Assessment Policies

* You are expected to submit all assessments
* You will not be eligible for a passing grade unless you have submitted at least 65% of the assessments (by weightage)
* No extensions
* If you miss an assessment or fail to submit before the deadline, you get a zero

---

# Academic Integrity

* No plagiarism
* No copying from each other
* No sharing of answers
* Both provider and copier of answers get the same penalty
  * Minor violations: zero grade for the entire assessment
  * Egregious violations: U grade for the course

---

# AI Policy

* OK to use judiciously, with caution and with attribution
* It is your responsibility to ensure correctness
* There may be long-term consequences to becoming dependent on AI
* Detailed AI Policy will be communicated shortly

---

# Other Points

* Bring your laptop to every session
* Ensure your laptop has sufficient charge to last the class
