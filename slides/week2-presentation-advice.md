# Structure: Possible template

- **Background/Introduction** (2-3 slides)
  - Broad overview, Explain/define essential ideas/concepts

- **Important discussion points** (2-3 slides)
  - Highlight important points based on your research

- **Reflections** (1-2 slides)
  - What does your group feel about the topic? Why is the topic relevant for
    information science students?

- **Summary** (1 slide)
  - Recap of important points

> You can organize your presentation differently as long as you include everything mentioned in the template

---

# Aesthetics and layout

- Have a consistent style/font/colour scheme

- Avoid text-heavy slides
  - Ensure there is adequate empty space
  - If necessary, have text appear one point at a time

- Avoid complete sentences (unless you are quoting someone)
  - Highlight important points/terms using bold font/different colour

---

# Presentation tips: Timing

- Budget for **60-90 seconds per slide**. More if the slide has complex
  ideas/graphs

- Speak slowly and clearly
  - Take your time for each point

- Avoid packing too much content
  - This will make you rush through the slide
  - Okay to end early, but you will be penalized if you exceed 10 minutes

---

# Presentation tips: Content

- Do not assume that your classmates are familiar with the topic

- Slide content should be self-explanatory
  - Audience should be able to follow even if they briefly stop listening to you

- Have informative titles
  - Purpose/point of having that slide

---

# Presentation tips: Practice

- Important to **rehearse**
  - You should be familiar with the slides and you will also have a sense for
    timing

- Do not read from a script during the presentation

- Penalty for insufficient practice and/or reading from a script
